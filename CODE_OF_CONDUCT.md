# Community Code of Conduct

This project wants a community that is critical, curious, respectful and safe for people who are still learning.

## Expected Behavior

- criticize ideas and work without humiliating people;
- explain problems and possible improvements when giving feedback;
- welcome beginners and reasonable questions;
- respect different levels of experience;
- credit other people's work;
- disclose conflicts of interest when relevant;
- disagree without harassment, threats or personal attacks;
- protect personal and confidential information;
- report serious problems through the appropriate project channel.

## Not Acceptable

- harassment or discrimination;
- threats or targeted intimidation;
- doxxing or publishing private information;
- plagiarism or knowingly misrepresenting authorship;
- vote manipulation, fake accounts or coordinated deception;
- sexual harassment or unwanted sexual attention;
- repeated personal attacks disguised as technical criticism;
- abusing moderator, maintainer or reviewer privileges.

## Learning Culture

A weak contribution is not a reason to ridicule its author.

Review should help answer:

- What is not working?
- Why does it matter?
- What can be learned?
- What would make the next version better?

Repeated bad-faith behavior is different from an honest mistake and may be moderated accordingly.

## Enforcement

Moderation should be proportionate, documented when appropriate and open to review for serious cases.

Possible actions include clarification, warning, temporary restriction or removal from project spaces, depending on severity and repetition.

Rules apply to contributors, reviewers, moderators and maintainers alike.
