# Learning and Education

This project is intended to become more than a game repository.

We want it to become a place where people can learn, contribute, build a real portfolio and leave a verifiable mark on the history of the project.

> **Build the world. Build your future.**

## A Place to Learn by Building

When contribution programs open, beginners and students should be able to work on small, real problems with clear requirements, review and feedback.

Possible areas include:

- programming and tools;
- game and level design;
- 3D modeling, rigging and animation;
- materials, environment art and UI/UX;
- sound, music and audiovisual production;
- writing, documentation and localization;
- QA, accessibility and security;
- biology, ecology, geography, architecture, economics and other fields that can help a believable living world.

Accepted work should preserve attribution and, when appropriate, provide verifiable evidence that contributors can reference in their portfolios.

## Culture

Beginners must be allowed to make mistakes without being humiliated.

Feedback should explain what can improve, why it matters and how to try again.

> A strong community is not created when everyone knows everything. It is created when people who know help people who are still learning.

The project should value listening, collaboration, respectful criticism, responsibility and the ability to teach others.

## Schools and Universities

In the future, we want to explore structured collaboration with schools, universities, technical courses, research groups and community programs.

Real project problems may eventually become suitable material for classes, extension projects, research, capstone work or supervised student teams when the legal, technical and educational structure is ready.

Formal internships, voluntary contributions and professional paid work must remain distinct paths with appropriate rules.

## Two Kinds of Legacy

The game is designed around the idea that the world remembers what players do.

The project should also remember the people who build that world.

Meaningful contributions can become part of a contributor's public history: what they built, what they learned, what they reviewed and how they helped others grow.

## Education Book

A second founding book is planned alongside the narrative books of the game.

The **Culture and Learning Book** is intended for students, teachers, mentors, schools and communities. It will explore how people can learn through real collaborative projects, build healthy teams, use mistakes as learning material, preserve authorship and grow from beginner to mentor.

This book is in foundation-stage planning and is not yet written.
