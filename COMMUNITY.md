# Community

This project began with a simple question: can a game be built with a community helping shape and sustain it from the beginning?

The first public phase is about gathering people around the world, story and core idea.

Community participation may include:

- discussing the world and story;
- proposing ideas;
- joining selected votes;
- helping evaluate names and visual directions;
- following the first prototype as it develops;
- preserving the history of important project decisions.

Community participation does not mean that every proposal becomes official. Major decisions must remain coherent with the project's current source of truth, technical feasibility and legal requirements.

The project will preserve attribution for meaningful community contributions whenever appropriate.

The first planned community decision is the final project name. See [NAME_THE_WORLD.md](NAME_THE_WORLD.md).

## Learning and Portfolio

The long-term community vision also includes a structured path for students, beginners, mentors, schools and universities.

The goal is for real accepted contributions to become useful learning experiences and, when appropriate, verifiable portfolio work with preserved attribution.

This program is not open yet. Its current principles are described in [LEARNING_AND_EDUCATION.md](LEARNING_AND_EDUCATION.md).
