# Project Roadmap — The Living Universe

> *"The world does not exist to serve the player. The world remembers. There is no destiny. There are choices."*

This roadmap outlines the official phases for the development of our realistic, persistent cooperative world and its transmedia literature.

---

## 🏛️ Phase 0 — Open Foundation (Current Phase)
The objective is to establish the core vision, community governance, and foundational lore before writing extensive game code.

- [x] Establish the Golden Rule: Pure Realism (grounded humans, physical nature, zero magic).
- [x] Launch open GitHub repository and documentation.
- [x] Launch the interactive web landing page with live GitHub sync.
- [x] Open the **"Name The World"** community initiative.
- [ ] Finalize the official world name and book title based on community proposals.
- [ ] Complete the foundational narrative draft for **Book Zero**.

---

## 🌲 Phase 1 — Playable Core Prototype (Godot 4)
Prove the fundamental gameplay loop in a tightly scoped, beautiful environment before expanding.

- [ ] Realistic human locomotion in third/first person with natural inertia and realistic stamina.
- [ ] Single handcrafted biome: dense temperate forest with realistic PBR shaders and natural lighting.
- [ ] Dynamic river with physical current and dangerous water crossing mechanics.
- [ ] Realistic tree felling with physical trunk falls and permanent stump/soil impact.
- [ ] Primitive survival gathering (timber, loose stones, natural binding fibers, clay).
- [ ] Basic modular log shelter construction and campfire warmth system.
- [ ] Cooperative local/LAN multiplayer for **2 to 5 players**.

---

## 🍃 Phase 2 — The Living World & Ecology
Introduce persistence and environmental consequence.

- [ ] Natural soil and vegetation recovery cycles over extended in-game seasons.
- [ ] Wildlife with realistic herd instincts, migration, and defensive behaviors.
- [ ] Weather systems (heavy rain affecting river volume, clothing weight, and body temperature).
- [ ] Structural decay: abandoned human settlements gradually rot and turn into overgrown ruins.

---

## ⚔️ Phase 3 — Persistent Clans & Territory
Transform isolated servers into interconnected living societies.

- [ ] Persistent community servers acting as distinct geographical clans.
- [ ] Regional resource specialization (hardwood valleys, mineral-rich highlands, fertile plains).
- [ ] Player-driven trade routes and organic inter-clan diplomacy.

---

## 📖 Phase 4 — The First Living Chronicle
Complete the full loop between the game and real-world literature.

- [ ] Curation of the most impactful player and clan events from Year 1 of active servers.
- [ ] Editorial synthesis into the official **Annual Chronicle: Year One**.
- [ ] Community members' names and clans immortalized in official published literature.
- [ ] Free digital distribution on the website + limited collector's physical edition.

---

> **Development Philosophy:** We do not publish unrealistic release deadlines. We build iteratively, in public, with quality and architectural discipline over rushed promises.
