# Culture and Learning Book

Status: **FOUNDATION / EARLY DEVELOPMENT**

This is the planned second founding book of the project.

While Book Zero introduces the fictional world, this book is about the real people who learn, teach and build together outside that world.

Its purpose is to help students, teachers, mentors, schools and communities use real collaborative projects as a place for learning and professional growth.

Core themes currently planned:

- learning by building;
- respectful technical and creative feedback;
- turning mistakes into learning;
- teamwork and responsibility;
- authorship and attribution;
- verifiable portfolio work;
- mentorship;
- healthy community culture;
- practical projects for classrooms and study groups;
- collaboration between schools, universities and open projects;
- the journey from beginner to contributor, reviewer and mentor.

> **Learn by building. Grow by contributing. Leave a legacy.**

The final title, structure, chapters and publication format are still open.
