## 📌 1. Executive Summary

The **Unnamed World Project** is an emerging transmedia intellectual property uniting two passionate, high-retention global markets:
1. **Persistent Survival & Cooperative Gaming:** A grounded, pure-realism world where nature is indifferent, physical consequences are permanent, and players cooperatively keep settlements and ecosystems alive.
2. **Collector's Literature & Publishing:** An official book series where real server history (clan wars, treaties, expeditions, and settlements) is curated annually into official physical hardcover books, imprinting player names into genuine literary history.

Built using **Godot Engine 4**, the project eliminates heavy third-party engine revenue royalties (unlike Unreal or Unity), maintaining an ultra-lean operational cost structure driven by open-source community contributions and architectural discipline.

## 📊 3. Revenue Drivers & Monetization Model

### A. High-Margin Collector's Hardcover Books (Primary Driver)
* **The Living Chronicles:** Published every 12 months. Each edition contains curated, edited accounts of real server history, authentic hand-drawn cartography, and community member rosters.
* **Economic Advantage:** Books are produced via premium Print-on-Demand and limited luxury runs (foil stamping, faux-leather, ribbon markers). Players purchase the books because **their own clan achievements and names are officially immortalized in print**.
* **Gross Margin:** 60% to 75% on physical book sales.

### B. Game Client & Server Ecosystem
* Accessible base game pricing on digital storefronts (Steam, itch.io) focusing on viral adoption rather than aggressive pay-to-win microtransactions.
* Dedicated private server hosting tools and community server licenses.
