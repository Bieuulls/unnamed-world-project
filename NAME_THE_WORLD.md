# Name This World

The project does not have a final name yet.

The community will be invited to suggest and help choose a name that can represent the game, Book Zero, future Chronicles and the wider project identity.

## What Makes a Strong Candidate

A proposed name should:

- be short and memorable;
- be easy to pronounce and type;
- work internationally, especially in Portuguese, English and Spanish;
- feel distinctive enough to become a recognizable brand;
- fit a realistic world built around choices, consequences, community and memory;
- work for a game, books and future Chronicles;
- avoid numbers, unnecessary punctuation and confusing spelling.

## Before a Name Can Become Official

Community popularity alone is not enough. Finalists must also pass practical checks, including:

- conflicts with existing games, companies and major entertainment properties;
- trademark availability and professional legal review when required;
- reasonable domain availability;
- reasonable availability of consistent social-media identities;
- unintended meanings or pronunciation problems in major target languages.

The winning public vote therefore identifies the preferred candidate. The name becomes official only after the required availability and legal checks are completed.

## Official Selection Flow

1. Community proposals.
2. Eligibility and originality screening.
3. Shortlist of the strongest candidates.
4. Availability and conflict checks.
5. Community vote among eligible finalists.
6. Final legal and registration checks.
7. Official announcement and registration.

## Proposal Rules

When the proposal discussion opens:

- submit one name per comment so each candidate can be discussed clearly;
- include a short explanation of why the name fits the project;
- include a pronunciation guide when the spelling may be unclear;
- mention known meanings in Portuguese, English, Spanish or other languages;
- do not submit a name copied from another game, company, book, film or established brand;
- do not purchase domains, reserve social accounts or file trademarks on behalf of the project;
- reactions during the proposal phase show interest but do not count as the final vote.

Submitting a suggestion does not create automatic ownership, partnership, revenue rights or authority to register the name. Meaningful participation and the origin of the selected proposal should be credited in the project's historical record whenever appropriate.

## Screening

Before a candidate can reach the final vote, it must be checked for:

- similarity to existing games and entertainment properties;
- obvious trademark conflicts;
- domain and social-identity practicality;
- pronunciation and unintended meanings;
- fit with the game, books and future Chronicles;
- ability to remain useful if the project grows beyond its first region or prototype.

Screening removes candidates that cannot safely or realistically become the official name. It is not the final popularity vote.

## Final Vote

The final vote will use only screened candidates. The voting post must state:

- the eligible finalists;
- who can vote;
- the opening and closing time;
- how ties are handled;
- which final legal and registration checks still apply.

The community vote selects the preferred eligible candidate. The result becomes official only after the project completes the remaining legal, domain, social-identity and registration checks.

## Registration and Announcement

Domains, official social accounts, trademark applications and related registrations must be created under the founder's control or a future legal entity controlled by the founder.

The final name should be announced only after the required registrations and reservations are safely underway.

The proposal opening date and voting dates will be announced after the repository becomes public and the first community has had a reasonable opportunity to join.
