# Story Foundation

![AI-assisted realistic character concept art](assets/concept/04-human-characters.png)

> **CONCEPT ART - AI ASSISTED. Not an in-game screenshot.**

The story is still being created.

The first book will explain the world before the players arrive.

The central philosophy is:

The land does not hate you.

It simply does not care.

One possible opening idea currently being explored:

A group of people travels into an unknown territory looking for a new beginning.

Early in the journey, they must cross a river.

An experienced man enters the water.

He knows how to swim.

He has a family waiting for him.

The river takes him anyway.

The important idea is not that the river is evil.

The river simply continues flowing.

The story must focus on:

- human choices
- consequences
- survival
- community
- knowledge
- mistakes
- recovery
- legacy

Avoid prophecy.

Avoid chosen heroes.

Avoid destiny as the explanation for events.

People become important because of what they choose to do.
